## Bio

I studied art and design at Missouri State University without any sense of 
direction.  I was mostly lost - floating from one medium to the next, never 
finding a single way of mark making engaging enough to hold my interest. I 
engaged with them all, exploring, and found each process offered its own 
insights, experiences, and ways of expressing.

My current artwork continues this restlessness and exploration.
